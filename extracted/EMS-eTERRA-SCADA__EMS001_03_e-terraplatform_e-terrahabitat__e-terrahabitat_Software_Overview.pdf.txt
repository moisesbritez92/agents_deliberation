A pictorial view of the solution architecture is above. While limited in scope, the 
diagram shows that users and EMS machines are defined in Active Directory, 
and these users and machines authenticate to AD within the domain context. A 
user named Ray Santini logs in to WebFG Client and authenticates to AD and 
the WebFG Server. WebFG server passes the authenticated identity to PERMIT 
and PERMIT subsequently looks up access permissions for Ray. Ray may have 
a slightly different name in PERMIT. e.g. Ray’s middle name is added in the 
PERMIT database. PERMIT does the name mapping to find the correct name, 
account, and permissions.
